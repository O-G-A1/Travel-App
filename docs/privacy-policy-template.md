## Information and use
Voyara is a Nigerian ride-sharing and traveler-connection service. Depending on the features enabled, it may process account email, display name, account type (passenger or driver), phone number, home state, profile photo, vehicle details, ride locations and times, seat requests, fares in NGN, messages, follows, community posts/comments/likes, and identity-verification submissions. Do not collect real identity documents until reviewer access, provider checks, retention periods, and deletion procedures have been configured and disclosed. In the current build, identity documents and face photos are stored in private Supabase Storage and are visible only to the submitting account; automated face matching and a reviewer dashboard are not implemented. Profile photos and public profile fields are available to signed-in users. Phone numbers, identity files, and vehicle registration numbers must remain private. With Supabase configured, cloud records are processed in the operator's Supabase project and protected by database/storage access policies. Without Supabase, there is no shared user directory, account registration, or live ride service. No analytics are active by default.
